# Building landmark production replacements v2

Generated as four separate assets with the built-in GPT image-generation tool.
The v1 building landmark atlas was used only as a rendering-style reference.

All prompts requested a single front-elevation, low-resolution game sprite on a
flat chroma-key background, with no text, logo, watermark, surrounding room,
ground plane, cast shadow, or extra objects.

## Red service door

```text
One battered late-Soviet institutional service door. Faded oxblood-red painted
steel in a dark utilitarian frame, chipped enamel, shallow stamped ribs, a small
wired-glass inspection pane, black Bakelite lever and lock plate, and a blank
cream enamel inventory plate. Functional mass-produced construction; no brass,
hotel ornament, panic bar, barn bracing, or modern electronic lock. Flat solid
#00ff00 chroma-key background.
```

## Cracked wall

```text
One tall narrow patch of severely cracked late-Soviet institutional wall.
Peeling dirty plaster and faded limewash reveal coarse aggregate concrete,
rusty reinforcing bars, a small rough brick repair, and one mismatched rectangular
patch. No wooden lath. Flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background.
```

## Soda-water dispenser

```text
One late-Soviet AT-series carbonated-water vending machine. A plain pale

two simple selection buttons, one recessed dark dispensing niche containing a
faceted communal glass, and a visible rinse grate. No packaged products,
touchscreen, branding, letters, or numbers. Flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key
background.
```

## Aquarium

```text
One modest late-Soviet institutional aquarium. A rectangular tank in a visibly
welded dark-painted angle-iron frame, separate sheet-metal lamp, exposed air
tubing and bubbler, slightly cloudy blue-green water, plain gravel, sparse
elodea-like plants, and exactly two muted fish. Opaque illustrated glass and
water; no modern molded-plastic hood or retail base. Flat solid #ff00ff
chroma-key background.
```

## Shared rendering direction

Crisp hand-painted low-resolution 2D game art matching the v1 atlas; perfectly
straight-on front elevation; bold readable silhouette; sparse wear that survives

lighting; restrained late-Soviet material palette; no photorealism or 3D render.

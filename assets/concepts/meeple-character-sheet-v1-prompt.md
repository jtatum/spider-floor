# Meeple character reference sheet v1

Generated with the built-in GPT image-generation tool as art-direction
reference. The shipped passengers remain deterministic canvas drawings; this
bitmap is not loaded by the game or copied into the production build.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: game character reference sheet for tiny code-drawn canvas meeples
Primary request: Design a coherent lineup of eight civilian character
silhouettes for a darkly comic elevator game set in an aging late-Soviet
institutional building. Include two ordinary residents with clearly different
coat shapes, a woman with a practical tied headscarf and shopping bag, a worker
in a soft cap with a satchel, a VIP in a longer structured overcoat with a tiny
playful crown marker, a generous tipper visibly raising a coin purse or large
coin, a nervous rider with hunched shoulders and clasped hands, and a mover
bracing a large battered suitcase.
Subject: Full-body adult civilians, modest everyday clothing, varied ages and
body shapes, varied skin tones. Every gameplay role must be distinguishable by
silhouette without relying on color.
Style/medium: Crisp hand-painted 2D game character concept art with near-black
keylines, restrained flat shapes, two or three levels of subtle fabric shading,
slightly chunky proportions, and the same warm muted palette as a sepia/plum
industrial game. Designed to be simplified into readable 20–40 pixel canvas
sprites.
Composition/framing: One horizontal character lineup, straight-on or very
slight three-quarter stance, all feet on one baseline, generous even spacing,
each full body visible, no overlaps.
Scene/backdrop: Plain warm charcoal-gray studio backdrop.
Lighting/mood: Dim institutional overhead light; humane, weary, gently funny
rather than grim.
Color palette: Oxblood, faded blue, bottle green, tobacco brown, mustard, plum,
cream, near-black.
Materials/textures: Worn wool coats, simple cotton scarf, scuffed leather
satchel and suitcase, sparse readable seams and patches.
Constraints: Civilian clothing only; no military uniforms, no propaganda
imagery, no national emblems, no logos, no labels, no text, no watermark. No
photorealism, no 3D render, no glossy modern fashion, no cast shadows. Avoid
exaggerated ethnic stereotypes or caricature. Prioritize strong silhouettes and
props that survive extreme downscaling.
```
